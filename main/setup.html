Wi-Fi setup ESP-Sim

Connect a network

You're already on the board's own network. Join a 2.4 GHz network and the board restarts on it: this sheet goes away and the control pages are served on that network. If the network or password is wrong, the board comes back as its own access point: reconnect and try again.

Or keep the board offline

The simulators work with no internet. Only the clock is unset, so the pod starts at its own uptime and a phone that checks it may show an odd date.

Test equipment only. Simulated data, no insulin.
