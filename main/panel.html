How glucose is simulated

Virtual person

The virtual person eats on their own (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks from a list of foods), treats their own lows and sometimes exercises; their glucose follows the insulin they get. “From the simulated pump” means only what the pod, YpsoPump or patch delivers, so the AID's decisions show in the glucose. A toy model for testing software, not a physiological simulator.

Fixed

One value with a trend; every reading repeats it. The glucose the app sees is this value.

Load a trace

The file is read here, in your browser: only the glucose values, one every 5 minutes, go to the board. Names, notes and times stay behind. Readings are lined up on a 5-minute grid; a missing one stays missing (the app sees a gap). mmol/L is converted; LOW and HIGH become 40 and 400. Loading a trace replaces the one on the board and clears its log.
Replays the trace one reading every 5 minutes. Whatever the pump delivers, the app sees the same glucose, so two builds of an algorithm can be run against it and their logs compared: each line has the run, the trace position, and what the pump did (temp basals, boluses, insulin since the last reading). The trace waits while the sensor warms up or is not OK. Times of day are today's: for a like-for-like run, start at the same time of day or use flat schedules. A pod reports its boluses and when a temp basal runs, not its rate. Test equipment: use only a test phone that controls no real pump or pod.
