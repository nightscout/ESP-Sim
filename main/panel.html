Unacknowledged command

The ESP32 reboots at the next command that changes delivery, leaving the phone without a response, then comes back as the same pod. Watch how the app recovers.

Pod type

Switching forgets the current pod. Omnipod 5 pairs with apps whose OmnipodKit supports it, such as Loop 3.14.7 and Trio 1.0 or later.

New pod

Clears pairing and delivery: the next phone pairs it as a fresh pod. Deactivating the pod from the app does the same.

Wi-Fi
